## What is covered

- `data/raw/renisus_knowledge_graph_ids.csv` (species, search terms and Knowledge Graph IDs compiled by the authors);
- results and classification tables produced by the scripts (for example `its_results_*.csv`, the recategorized and deduplicated
  Mercado Livre tables, the registry classification derived from ANVISA).

## What is NOT covered (third-party content)

The following material comes from third-party sources and remains subject to the terms of those sources. It is provided only to allow
the reproduction of this study:

- Google Trends values (Google);
- Mercado Livre listing titles, IDs, links and prices (Mercado Livre and the sellers);
- ANVISA medicine registry information (ANVISA, public database) and the RENISUS list (Ministério da Saúde, 2009).
